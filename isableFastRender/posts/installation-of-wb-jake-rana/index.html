Installation of WB Jake Rana

23 May 2026

The installation of the Worshipful Master and Officers of Vermillion Lodge No. 68 was a momentous occasion, held on May 23rd, 2026.

This year, the Parkland District joint installation of officers brought together three Brother Lodges:

  • Vermillion Lodge No. 68 (Dauphin, MB) – Installing WB Jake Rana as Worshipful Master.
  • Prince of Wales Lodge No. 14 (Minnedosa, MB) – Installing Bro. Thedwinston Paris as Worshipful Master.
  • Russell Lodge No. 62 (Russell, MB) – Installing RW Bro. Wes Osborne as Worshipful Master.

The ceremony was attended by distinguished members of the Grand Lodge of Manitoba, lodge members, and their families and friends. We extend our warmest congratulations to WB Jake Rana and all the newly installed officers across the Parkland District. We look forward to a successful and inspiring year ahead of Masonic brotherhood, community dedication, and mutual improvement.
